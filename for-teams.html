The Dynamics of Power

When alignment dissolves, so does impact.

In high-consequence environments, technical brilliance is table stakes. The real friction exists in the "unspoken room"—the place where hesitation, misaligned incentives, and defensive posture slow down the speed of decision-making.

These dynamics are not solved by another offsite or a generic personality test. They are solved by bringing the right diagnostic into the room to expose the structural and relational gaps preventing peak execution.

The Team Performance Framework

The Six Conditions of Team Performance

A

Alignment

Does this team share a clear picture of where we are going — and why now?

T

Trust

Can people on this team say the hard thing without managing the reaction first?

T

Truth

What does this team know but is not yet saying?

U

Understanding

What is driving the friction underneath what is being said?

N

Navigation

How does this team move through tension to a decision everyone holds?

E

Execution

Does what was decided in the room still hold on Friday?
