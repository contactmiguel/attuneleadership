STRATEGIC LEADERSHIP COUNCIL

The decisions that matter most cannot be discussed inside your organization.

A small, confidential peer advisory forum for senior leaders operating under complexity and consequence. Structured around the A.T.T.U.N.E.™ framework — not peer opinion, but disciplined diagnostic process.

Inquire About the Council The Framework

The weight of high-consequence leadership is often borne in silence.

As you ascend the organizational hierarchy, your circle of true confidants shrinks. Every internal conversation is layered with politics, perception, and professional stakes. The strategic challenges you face require a level of raw honesty that the corporate environment rarely permits.

Standard executive coaching provides the mirror, but often lacks the peer-to-peer friction that sharpens strategic edge. You don't just need a listener; you need a room where your diagnostic assumptions are tested by those who understand the altitude you operate at.

A Different Architecture

Why the Council is Not a Networking Group

architecture

Structured, not open.

We bypass the casual "catch-up." Every convening is governed by the ATTUNE diagnostic protocols, ensuring every hour is spent on high-leverage strategic inquiry.

troubleshoot

Diagnostic, not advisory.

We don't trade "best practices" or unsolicited advice. We ask the questions that expose the root cause of organizational friction, allowing you to find the precise solution.

verified_user

Confidential by design.

The Council is limited to non-competing peers. The physical and digital space is curated for total discretion, protecting the integrity of your strategic planning.

Program Logistics & Operational Cadence

Excellence requires consistency. The Council operates on a rigorous annual cycle designed to integrate with the demands of an executive schedule.

01

A small cohort

Groups are strictly limited to 8–10 executives to ensure meaningful airtime and deep relational trust.

02

Quarterly convenings

Full-day immersion sessions held in discrete, off-site locations or high-security digital environments.

03

ATTUNE-facilitated sessions

Led by Claudia, every session moves through a specific diagnostic track tailored to the group's real-time challenges.

04

Commitments, not insights

The session doesn't end with "ideas," it ends with a strategic commitment and a peer-backed accountability framework.

Who Belongs in the Room?

The Council is curated for leaders who possess high levels of responsibility and a willingness to be challenged.

Membership is by invitation or application only.

  • — C-Suite, Senior Executives, or Founders of organizations with significant operational complexity.
  • — Willingness to offer and receive radical candor within the diagnostic framework.
  • — Commitment to the quarterly cadence and inter-session accountability.
  • — High-stakes decision makers who value the separation of peer advisory from business development.

The Veil of Discretion

The efficacy of the Council rests entirely on the safety of the space. Every participant signs a comprehensive confidentiality agreement. What is shared stays in the room — including from Claudia. We create a vacuum of privacy where the true work of leadership can finally occur without the shadow of external perception.
