A

Alignment

DIAGNOSTIC

"Does this team share a clear picture of where we are going — and why now?"

Shared directionSpeed without alignment is movement in the wrong direction.

Alignment is not agreement on every detail — it is a shared picture of where the team is going and why now. Without it, capable people pull hard in different directions, and effort becomes noise.

T

Trust

DIAGNOSTIC

"Can people on this team say the hard thing without managing the reaction first?"

The foundation of velocityTeams that cannot risk honesty spend their capacity on self-protection instead of execution.

Trust is the condition that makes speed possible. When people can say the hard thing without managing the reaction first, the team's full capacity goes to the work — not to defending positions.

T

Truth

DIAGNOSTIC

"What does this team know but is not yet saying?"

Naming realityWhat the team cannot name, it cannot solve. Polite agreement is the slowest path to resolution.

Every team knows things it is not yet saying. Truth is the discipline of naming reality in the room — because what stays unsaid does not disappear. It goes underground and slows everything down.

U

Understanding

DIAGNOSTIC

"What is driving the friction underneath what is being said?"

Beneath the surfaceThe presenting conflict is rarely the real one.

High-performing teams read behavior before they respond. Understanding is the capacity to see what is driving the friction underneath what is being said — and to address that, not the symptom.

N

Navigation

DIAGNOSTIC

"How does this team move through tension to a decision everyone holds?"

Decisions under pressureA team that avoids disagreement makes decisions that dissolve.

Navigation is the capacity to commit even when the answer was contested — to move through tension to a decision everyone holds, rather than a consensus no one believes in.

E

Execution

DIAGNOSTIC

"Does what was decided in the room still hold on Friday?"

Accountability that holdsExecution fails at the handoff when ownership is assumed rather than named.

Decisions are only as good as what happens after the meeting ends. Execution is accountability that holds — ownership named in the room, and commitments that still stand days later.

C-SUITE ARCHITECTURE

Mastering the Dynamics of 'The Room'

Team performance happens in the quiet moments between meetings, in the tension of a disagreement, and in the clarity of a shared direction. Our framework provides the diagnostic tools to measure these invisible conditions.

By applying Team A.T.T.U.N.E.™, executive teams move from reactive firefighting to deliberately building the human conditions that make strategy executable.
