THE DEFINITION
"A Bespoke Retreat is not a planning session. It is not a team-building event. It is a structured behavioral working retreat."

In the room, we bypass the tactical noise to address the underlying dynamics that either accelerate or sabotage strategic execution. We facilitate the high-consequence conversations most teams are avoiding.

Built on The Five Behaviors

Our retreats integrate Patrick Lencioni's Five Behaviors model — the gold standard for high-performing teams — with the ATTUNE Framework's relational diagnostics.

What changes in two days

Trust Architecture

Move from surface-level collegiality to the kind of trust that allows for genuine strategic dissent.

Conflict Resolution Norms

Establish shared protocols for productive disagreement that accelerate, rather than stall, decisions.

Shared Accountability Language

A common vocabulary for naming what is and isn't working — without the weight of hierarchy distorting the message.

Team Results Orientation

Align personal incentives with collective outcomes so "we" replaces "my function" in every key decision.

Who This Is For

Teams at a Strategic Inflection Point

Post-Series C, pre-IPO, post-acquisition, or entering a new market phase. The old operating model no longer fits.

C-Suites with Accumulated Friction

Teams that function well on paper but feel the weight of unsaid things slowing every decision.

New Leadership Teams

Newly formed executive teams that need to build trust quickly and establish operating norms before pressure arrives.

Leaders Who Value Discretion

All retreats are conducted under strict confidentiality. What happens in the room stays in the room.
